*Watchmen* is a 12-issue limited series written by Alan Moore, illustrated
by Dave Gibbons, and colored by John Higgins, originally published by DC
Comics between 1986 and 1987.

Set in an alternate 1985 where masked vigilantes are a part of history and
the Cold War looms large, it follows a group of retired and disillusioned
superheroes as they investigate the murder of one of their own.
