*Saga* is an ongoing space-opera fantasy comic book series written by
Brian K. Vaughan and illustrated by Fiona Staples, published by Image
Comics since 2012.

It tells the story of Alana and Marko, two star-crossed lovers from
opposite sides of a galactic war, and their struggle to raise their
daughter Hazel amid the conflict.
